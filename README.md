### ⚖️ CaseSage: Analysis of Indian Legal Documents
**November 2024 – May 2025** | [GitHub](https://github.com/c-surendra-kumar/legal-insight-provider)

Comprehensive legal NLP system with four integrated modules:

**1. 📝 Summarization Module**
- Hybrid extractive-abstractive approach using Longformer
- Named Entity Recognition (NER) integration to preserve critical legal entities
- Enables rapid comprehension of lengthy court judgments

**2. 🔍 Case Similarity & Precedent Retrieval**
- Knowledge graph-based approach capturing relationships between legal concepts, parties, and statutes
- **Result**: 40% accuracy improvement over traditional keyword-based search

**3. ❓ Question Answering System**
- RAG pipeline powered by DeepSeek-v3 for natural language queries
- Responses grounded in actual case law to prevent hallucination
- **Result**: 65% reduction in lookup time compared to manual search

**4. 🏷️ Semantic Segmentation**
- LSTM-based automated extraction and tagging of legal sections (facts, arguments, judgment)
- Eliminates manual annotation requirements
